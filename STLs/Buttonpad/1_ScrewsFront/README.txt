AstroCan X Buttonpad - screws from the front
============================================
www.printed-droid.com

Case for the AstroCan Buttonpad (PCB v5, 41.91 x 56.515 x 1.6 mm, 4x5 buttons, 0.91" OLED).
Short screws from the front: through the lid and the PCB into the bottom shell.

FILES
-----
  Buttonpad_Lid.stl            lid with button holes (countersunk), display window - print top side down
  Buttonpad_Lid_Pockets.stl    alternative lid: one recess per button (for shorter button caps) - top side down
  Buttonpad_BottomShell.stl    bottom shell (USB-C, switch, 2 side buttons, "AstroCan X" on the back) - floor down

-----
  - Hardware: 3x M2 x 12 countersunk screws, from the front.
  - Pilot holes in the bottom shell are 1.7 mm, 7 mm deep.
  - Display window: right edge (seen from the front, display on top) 1 mm shorter than before.
