ASSEMBLY (strut, seen from the back of the RadarEye)
  1. Display into the pocket, flex cable through the flex notch (left).
  2. Strut: right end into the slot in the right pocket wall, left end down into the notch behind the flex cutout.
  3. 1x M2 countersunk approx. 8 mm into the pilot hole (d1.7).
  4. Strut underside is 5.2 mm behind the display seat - shim the rest if the display is thinner.
  5. Mount on the dome as before: 3x M3 approx. 20 mm through card holder and dome into the ScreenMod.

Dimensions: see BUILD_SETTINGS.txt (lens-axis frame, strut position, measured dome clearance).
