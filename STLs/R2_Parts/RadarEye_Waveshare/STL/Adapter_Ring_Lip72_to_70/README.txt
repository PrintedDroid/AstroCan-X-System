RadarEye ScreenMod - adapter ring old lip -> new lip
=====================================================
www.printed-droid.com

For ScreenMods printed with the OLD display lip (opening d72.2): this ring is laid into the display pocket
on top of the lip and reduces the opening to the NEW lip size d70.2. The display (d73) then rests on 1.4 mm instead of 0.4 mm.

FILES
  RadarEye_LipAdapterRing_ID70.2_OD76.6_0.5mm.stl   inner d70.2, outer d76.6, thickness 0.5 mm - print flat
  Preview_Ring.png

NOTES
  - Outer d76.6 = display pocket d77 minus 0.2 mm clearance per side.
  - 0.5 mm thick: the display sits 0.5 mm further back - check the strut / shim if you use the strut version.
