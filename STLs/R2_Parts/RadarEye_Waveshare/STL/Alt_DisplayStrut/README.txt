RadarEye ScreenMod - ALTERNATIVE with display strut
====================================================
www.printed-droid.com

The display is clamped from the back by a flat strut (2 mm). Remaining gap to the display back is shimmed.

FILES
  RadarEye_ScreenMod_Ledge1mm_Strut.stl   ScreenMod, lip d70.2 x 1 mm, with strut slot + notch
  RadarEye_ScreenMod_Ledge2mm_Strut.stl   same with 2 mm lip (choose one)
  RadarEye_DisplayStrut.stl               flat strut 94.4 x 10 x 2 mm - print flat

PREVIEWS
  Preview_Back_WithStrut.png, Preview_Back_Iso_WithStrut.png, Preview_Back_Iso_NoStrut.png

ASSEMBLY (seen from the back)
  1. Put the display into the pocket (flex cable through the flex notch, left).
  2. Push the right end of the strut into the slot in the right pocket wall (5 mm below the back edge).
  3. Lower the left end into the notch behind the flex cable cutout.
  4. Screw: 1x M2 countersunk approx. 8 mm (pilot hole d1.7, 8 mm deep).

NOTES
  - Strut underside 5.2 mm behind the display seat (lip). Strut top 0.3 mm in front of the dome surface
    (the dome is only 7.5 mm behind the seat at the eye centre - a T-beam or a deeper strut would hit the dome).
  - Display thinner than 5.2 mm at the back: shim the gap (e.g. foam or printed spacer).
  - Flex cable notch is 1 mm shorter on the left (outer edge moved 1 mm inwards).
