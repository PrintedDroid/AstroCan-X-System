Lightsaber Remote - Standard PrintVertical
==================================================
www.printed-droid.com

Standard d42, printed standing, split above the controls + screw-on EmitterTube.
Ends like the Saber Mini Remote: thread d35 (pitch 2.5) at both ends, LockRing against the shoulder, end piece screwed on.

FILES
-----
  Saber_Vertical_FrontShell.stl            front half - all modules sit in here
  Saber_Vertical_BackShell.stl             back half
  Saber_Vertical_FrontShell/BackShell_Grenade / _Ribs / _Panels   grip alternatives (use as a pair, see Preview_Grips.png)
  Saber_Vertical_EmitterTube.stl           screws onto the shells above the controls, carries the emitter end
  Saber_Vertical_LockRing.stl              print 1x - lock ring for the emitter end
  Saber_Vertical_LockRing_Cone.stl         print 1x - lock ring for the pommel end (cone towards the 45 deg shoulder)
  Saber_Vertical_EmitterHead_Classic_Short.stl   emitter (more designs, short and long: ../7_Ends_Standard)
  Saber_Vertical_PommelHead_Classic.stl    pommel, closed - unscrew it for USB charging (more designs: ../7_Ends_Standard)
  Saber_Vertical_BlindPlug.stl             plug for unused side-button holes (as needed)

PREVIEWS
--------
  Preview_Assembly / Front / Back / Section / PrintParts / Grips

ASSEMBLY
--------
  1. Modules into the FrontShell, close with the BackShell (4 alignment pegs are part of the FrontShell), 2x M2 countersunk screws.
  2. Screw the EmitterTube onto the joint thread above the controls.
  3. LockRing_Cone onto the pommel stud (cone towards the shell), LockRing onto the emitter stud, each up to the shoulder.
  4. Screw on pommel head and emitter head.

PRINTING
--------
  - All parts standing as exported. Only the underside of the control box needs support. Brim for the shells.
  - On/off switch: slide it into its pocket from the open (split) side of the FrontShell.
  - End parts (LockRing, heads) as exported, no supports. Threads: right-hand, pitch 2.5 mm.
