Lightsaber Remote - Standard, printed lying
===========================================
www.printed-droid.com

Ø42 hilt with control box, total length approx. 270 mm. Shells are printed lying on the split face.

FILES
-----
  Saber_BackShell.stl                                back half (mounting cover)
  Saber_BlindPlug.stl                                plug for unused side-button holes (as needed)
  Saber_DowelPin.stl                                 alignment pin between the shells - print 4x
  Saber_EmitterRing.stl                              emitter ring, screws on top
  Saber_FrontShell.stl                               front half - all modules sit in here
  Saber_PommelRing.stl                               pommel ring with USB hole, screws on the bottom

GRIP ALTERNATIVES (optional - replace FrontShell + BackShell as a pair)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Saber_FrontShell_Grenade / Saber_BackShell_Grenade   raised square pads (grenade grip)
  Saber_FrontShell_Ribs    / Saber_BackShell_Ribs      long raised ribs
  Saber_FrontShell_Panels  / Saber_BackShell_Panels    raised panels
  Patterns only on back and sides of the control section; control box, side buttons, switch, screws stay free.
  All pattern flanks 45 deg - prints like the plain shells, no extra supports.
  Preview_Grips.png shows all three (front and back).

-----
  - Shells: split face down on the bed.
  - Rings: thread opening down.
  - Hardware: 4x M2 countersunk screws approx. 12 mm (from the back), 4 dowel pins.
