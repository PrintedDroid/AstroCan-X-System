Lightsaber Remote - Slim, printed standing
==========================================
www.printed-droid.com

Ø38 hilt (Slim), shells printed standing (pommel down), split above the controls like 2_Standard_PrintVertical:
shells 198 mm high + screw-on EmitterTube (55 mm). New emitter ring with inner 45 deg cone, new pommel ring.
Total length approx. 264 mm.

FILES
  Saber_Slim_Vertical_FrontShell.stl   front half - all modules sit in here
  Saber_Slim_Vertical_BackShell.stl    back half
  Saber_Slim_Vertical_EmitterTube.stl  screws onto the shells, carries the emitter ring
  Saber_Slim_Vertical_EmitterRing.stl  emitter ring
  Saber_Slim_Vertical_PommelRing.stl   pommel ring with USB hole
  Saber_Slim_Vertical_BlindPlug.stl    plug for unused side-button holes (as needed)
  Saber_Slim_Vertical_DowelPin.stl     alignment pin - print 4x

GRIP ALTERNATIVES (optional - replace FrontShell + BackShell as a pair)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Saber_Slim_Vertical_FrontShell_Grenade / Saber_Slim_Vertical_BackShell_Grenade   raised square pads (grenade grip)
  Saber_Slim_Vertical_FrontShell_Ribs    / Saber_Slim_Vertical_BackShell_Ribs      long raised ribs
  Saber_Slim_Vertical_FrontShell_Panels  / Saber_Slim_Vertical_BackShell_Panels    raised panels
  Patterns only on back and sides of the control section; control box, side buttons, switch, screws stay free.
  All pattern flanks 45 deg - prints like the plain shells, no extra supports.
  Preview_Grips.png shows all three (front and back).

PREVIEWS

NOTES
  - All parts standing as exported; only the underside of the control box needs support. Use a brim for the shells.
  - Hardware: 4x M2 countersunk screws, 4 dowel pins. Threads: right-hand, pitch 2.5 mm.
  - On Slim the flutes stay on the shells (they are below the split), the EmitterTube starts with the neck.
