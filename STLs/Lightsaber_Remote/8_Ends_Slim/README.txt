Lightsaber Remote - modular ends, Slim (d38)
================================================
www.printed-droid.com

Screw-on end pieces in two parts, split after the grooves. Fits the Slim versions (5_Slim_PrintFlat, 6_Slim_PrintVertical):
the heads screw onto the existing threads of the shells / EmitterTube, so they replace EmitterRing / PommelRing.

  SLEEVE (ring)  slides loosely over the smooth collar of the thread stud
  HEAD           screws onto the thread and clamps the sleeve against the shoulder
Every head works with every sleeve of the same end - mix freely.

EMITTER (front, joystick side)
  Saber_Slim_EmitterSleeve_Classic / _Ribs / _Flutes      12 mm ring
  Saber_Slim_EmitterHead_Classic   flared collar with windows, notched lip (the current design)
  Saber_Slim_EmitterHead_Shroud    slim neck, 45 deg funnel to a wide disc with a ring of holes
  Saber_Slim_EmitterHead_Crown     octagon with recessed panels and crenellated top
  Saber_Slim_EmitterHead_Stepped   smooth shaft with 3 grooves, stepped lip

POMMEL (back, USB side)
  Saber_Slim_PommelSleeve_Classic / _Ribs / _Flutes       14 mm ring
  Saber_Slim_PommelHead_Classic    chamfer, groove, 8 round flutes (the current design, flat bottom)
  Saber_Slim_PommelHead_Grenade    stack of V-rings
  Saber_Slim_PommelHead_Facet      octagon with slots
  Saber_Slim_PommelHead_Spool      wide knurled foot, waisted

PREVIEWS
  Preview_PrintParts.png     all parts as they go on the print bed, with heights
  Preview_Combinations.png   example combinations on the hilt

PRINTING (all parts as exported, no supports - every overhang is 45 deg or less)
  - Emitter heads: thread opening down.  Pommel heads: flat bottom down.  Sleeves: either way.
  - Threads: right-hand, pitch 2.5 mm, same as the one-piece rings.

ASSEMBLY
  Slide the sleeve over the stud first, then screw the head on until it clamps the sleeve.
