Lightsaber Remote - Standard, printed standing
==============================================
www.printed-droid.com

Ø42 hilt with control box, shells printed standing (pommel down). The hilt is split above the controls:
shells 198 mm high + screw-on EmitterTube. New emitter ring with inner 45 deg cone, new pommel ring. Total length approx. 290 mm.

FILES
-----
  Saber_Vertical_BackShell.stl                       back half (mounting cover)
  Saber_Vertical_BlindPlug.stl                       plug for unused side-button holes (as needed)
  Saber_Vertical_DowelPin.stl                        alignment pin between the shells - print 4x
  Saber_Vertical_EmitterRing.stl                     emitter ring, screws on top
  Saber_Vertical_EmitterTube.stl                     screws onto the shells (top), carries the emitter ring
  Saber_Vertical_FrontShell.stl                      front half - all modules sit in here
  Saber_Vertical_PommelRing.stl                      pommel ring with USB hole, screws on the bottom

GRIP ALTERNATIVES (optional - replace FrontShell + BackShell as a pair)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Saber_Vertical_FrontShell_Grenade / Saber_Vertical_BackShell_Grenade   raised square pads (grenade grip)
  Saber_Vertical_FrontShell_Ribs    / Saber_Vertical_BackShell_Ribs      long raised ribs
  Saber_Vertical_FrontShell_Panels  / Saber_Vertical_BackShell_Panels    raised panels
  Patterns only on back and sides of the control section; control box, side buttons, switch, screws stay free.
  All pattern flanks 45 deg - prints like the plain shells, no extra supports.
  Preview_Grips.png shows all three (front and back).

NOTES
-----
  - 2026-10-16: shells and EmitterTube re-exported with a smaller joint thread (old tube had no wall over the thread).
    Old and new EmitterTube/shells do not fit each other - print both new.
  - All parts standing as exported (thread / pommel floor down).
  - Only the underside of the control box needs support.
  - Use a brim for the shells.
  - Hardware: 4x M2 countersunk screws approx. 12 mm, 4 dowel pins.
  - Threads: right-hand, pitch 2.5 mm.
