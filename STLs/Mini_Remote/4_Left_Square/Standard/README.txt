Mini Remote - left hand, square, Standard
=========================================
www.printed-droid.com

AstroCan X Mini Remote case, left-hand version, square lid edges, normal bottom shell.

FILES
-----
  MiniRemote_L_BottomShell.stl                       bottom with rear joystick hole
  MiniRemote_L_BottomShell_NoJoystick.stl            bottom without rear joystick hole
  MiniRemote_L_NavCap.stl                            cap for the navigation button
  MiniRemote_L_TopShell.stl                          upper housing (lid with all openings)

NOTES
-----
  - TopShell: lid down on the bed.
  - BottomShell: floor down on the bed.
  - NavCap: head down.
  - Pick ONE BottomShell: with rear joystick hole or NoJoystick.
  - Hardware: 5x M2 countersunk screws (sides + USB end).
