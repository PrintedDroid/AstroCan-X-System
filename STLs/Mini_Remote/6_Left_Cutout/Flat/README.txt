Mini Remote - left hand, cutout, Flat
=====================================
www.printed-droid.com

AstroCan X Mini Remote case, left-hand version, rounded, flat lid with joystick cutout, flat back: the top shell walls go all the way down, the bottom plate sits inside.

FILES
-----
  MiniRemote_L_Cutout_BottomShell_Flat.stl           bottom with rear joystick hole
  MiniRemote_L_Cutout_BottomShell_Flat_NoJoystick.stl bottom without rear joystick hole
  MiniRemote_L_Cutout_NavCap.stl                     cap for the navigation button
  MiniRemote_L_Cutout_TopShell_Flat.stl              upper housing (lid with all openings)

-----
  - TopShell: lid down on the bed.
  - BottomShell: floor down on the bed.
  - NavCap: head down.
  - Pick ONE BottomShell: with rear joystick hole or NoJoystick.
  - Hardware: 5x M2 countersunk screws (sides + USB end).
