Saber Mini Remote - left hand, joystick island
==============================================
www.printed-droid.com

Mini Remote (left-hand version) built into a Ø42 hilt, raised joystick island.

  SaberMiniRemote_BottomShell_L_NoScrews.stl         option: no side screws (lock rings hold the shells), guide tabs kept

  SaberMiniRemote_LockRing.stl                       lock ring - print 2x, one in front of each end

NOTES
-----
  - TopShell: split face down, supports under the lid / openings.
  - BottomShell: needs supports (round underside).
  - Emitter, lock ring: standing as exported, no supports.
  - GripTube (pommel down) needs supports for the grip grooves - support-free: 0_Options/GripTube_PommelDown or _OpenDown.
  - Pick ONE pair of shells: with screws, or both *_NoScrews (the lock rings hold the shells together).
  - Assembly: shells with 4x M2 countersunk screws (approx. 6-8 mm; not for NoScrews), then lock rings to the shoulder, then emitter and grip tube.
  - Threads: right-hand, pitch 2.5 mm.
