Saber Mini Remote - options
===========================
www.printed-droid.com

Alternative parts that fit all versions: short emitter, grip tubes that print without supports.

FILES
-----
  SaberMiniRemote_EmitterRing.stl                    emitter, short (option)
  SaberMiniRemote_GripTube_OpenDown.stl              grip tube for printing thread opening down (option)
  SaberMiniRemote_GripTube_PommelDown.stl            grip tube for printing standing on the pommel (option)

PREVIEWS
--------
  Preview_PrintParts.png                             all parts as they go on the print bed, with heights

NOTES
-----
  - TopShell: split face down, supports under the lid / openings.
  - BottomShell: needs supports (round underside).
  - Emitter, lock ring: standing as exported, no supports.
  - The standard GripTube (version folders) needs supports for its grip grooves. Use one of these instead:
      GripTube_PommelDown: flat pommel on the bed (45 deg chamfer), upper groove flanks 45 deg -> no supports.
      GripTube_OpenDown:   thread opening on the bed, lower groove flanks 45 deg -> no supports.
                           Since 2026-10-15 with a flat pommel top (the old domed top gave bad last layers).
  - Assembly: shells with 4x M2 countersunk screws (approx. 6-8 mm), then lock rings to the shoulder, then emitter and grip tube.
  - Threads: right-hand, pitch 2.5 mm.
